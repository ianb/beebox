# Message for Grady

Hey — so I added Telegram support to Callback Box, and I'm thinking about what to do with it. I could invite my family to a group. Set up a little isolated environment for them. And then — why not a whole bunch of these? Each one for a different group of people, each with its own group chat.

## The group thing is the interesting part

I also made a web chat interface, a bit richer than what Telegram can do. And I was thinking — should that be one-on-one chats that happen to work in the same space? I think the chat should be group chats as well, in every direction. Not just sharing results — sharing the process. Everyone sees what everyone does.

When somebody does something useful, everyone else learns how to do it too. You get a sense of what's possible. And most of the privacy issues get way simpler, because there's never a question about what's private — the expectation is that everyone sees everything. If you accidentally talk to the wrong box, you'll notice immediately because you'll see a different group's messages and members — not the group you intended.

## Multiple front ends

I think there's a place for different interfaces beyond chat. For example, in a family box:

- A family calendar (maybe syncing with Google Calendar)
- A to-do list or something more ad hoc
- An HTML dashboard page that the agent regularly updates — put it on a tablet in the kitchen

These are all just different views into the same box.

## Where it's at

This would still be a pretty manual setup process — not trying to polish onboarding. But the pieces are there. Could set up a group for you and me to try it out, or whatever makes sense.

The thing I'm excited about from a product/process standpoint: groups as the default, transparency as the feature, and multiple surfaces all looking into the same shared space.
