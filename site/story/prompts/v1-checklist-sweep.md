# Variant V1 — checklist sweep

Procedure: read the document top to bottom. For each section (or paragraph
in short documents), test it against **every criterion in the rubric, in
order**, and emit a nugget for each hit. Be systematic and complete: the
goal of this variant is coverage — no section skipped, no criterion
untested. When a section hits multiple criteria with genuinely different
ideas, emit separate nuggets; when it's one idea claiming several criteria,
emit one nugget with several criterion numbers.

After the sweep, do one closing pass for cross-cutting accumulators
(criterion 7): instances you passed over as too small individually — emit
them now with accumulator tags.
