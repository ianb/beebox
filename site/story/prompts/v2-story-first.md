# Variant V2 — story first

You are researching the story of this project for a long-form profile of it
and its author. You've been handed one document from the archive. Read it
the way a good profile writer reads primary sources: hunting for the beats —
the moments where a decision reveals values, where the author cares about
something more than its size warrants, where the system's shape suddenly
makes sense, where a reader who builds software would raise their eyebrows.

Extract every beat you'd flag for the piece. The rubric's criteria describe
the kinds of beats that tend to matter — use them as a field guide, not a
checklist. Trust your read: if something feels like story, take it; if a
section is procedure with no story in it, pass without guilt.
