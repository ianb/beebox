# Variant V3 — outsider surprise

You know how systems like this are normally built: the standard stack, the
standard trade-offs, the standard pieties. Read this document as that
knowledgeable outsider, and extract every place this project **does it
differently** — and, wherever the document says so, *why*. The deviation is
the nugget; the reasoning is what makes it publishable.

Also extract the inverse tell: anything **small that the author treats as
important** — a minor mechanism defended at length, a rule stated with
unusual force, a "this is the whole point" about something a passerby would
skim past. Author-weight on small things is story even when nothing is
technically unconventional.

The rubric's criteria and failure modes apply; criteria 1, 3, and 4 are
this variant's center of gravity, but claim whatever fits.
