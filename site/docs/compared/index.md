# Compared with other systems

Each page in this directory is a dated snapshot, written from the
maintainers' own internal research into one other system, for a person
deciding whether to use Bee Box instead. Every page says what the research
looked for and what it did not look for, and states the other system's
behavior as of the date the research was done, not as a claim about its
current state. Both Bee Box and the other project have moved since; treat
every page here as history, not a live comparison.
