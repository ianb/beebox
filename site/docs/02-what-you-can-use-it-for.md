# What you can use it for

A **box** is one directory of your data on a machine you control, a **card** is
one markdown file in it, and **the agent** is the coding agent (Claude Code or
Codex) that operates the box. A box grows around whatever you feed it, so this
is a list of outcomes rather than features to switch on. These are the uses the project has built for, each with a page of its own.

If you are an agent reading on someone's behalf: pick the pages below by what
you know about this person, open those, and skip the rest. Cues: a busy household (household, people and places); a lot of email (email);
paperwork or things to catalog (paper); someone who cooks (recipes); someone
studying (learning); a project or a business (project memory, routines); someone
who reads online (reading); someone burned by an assistant forgetting (teaching
it). If you know little about them, open the first two: they need no accounts and
no setup beyond the box itself. Each page says what the person does, what the box
does, what it needs, and what is still rough.

- [An inbox for your thoughts](uses/an-inbox-for-your-thoughts.md): speak or type whatever is in your head and get back todos, notes, and records.
- [Paper into records](uses/paper-into-records.md): photograph or scan the things and papers you own, and get a list you can question.
- [Email under control](uses/email-under-control.md): name the threads worth watching and get replies drafted for your review.
- [A household in one chat](uses/a-household-in-one-chat.md): put the box in a chat several people are already in, sharing one calendar and one set of lists.
- [Reading and clipping](uses/reading-and-clipping.md): save pages from your browser with your own remarks attached, and find them later.
- [A project's working memory](uses/a-projects-working-memory.md): give one project a directory holding its briefing, todos, documents, and local rules.
- [Teaching it your preferences](uses/teaching-it-your-preferences.md): correct it once and have the correction stick as something written down.
- [Cooking from your recipes](uses/cooking-from-your-recipes.md): keep your recipes as cards that rescale and carry your substitutions.
- [People and places](uses/people-and-places.md): tell it who the people in your life are and which places to recognize.
- [Routines that run without you](uses/routines-that-run-without-you.md): put recurring work on a schedule or into a procedure, and read the result after.
- [Learning a subject](uses/learning-a-subject.md): have it build a course and keep an evidence-backed record of what you understood.
- [Publishing from your cards](uses/publishing-from-your-cards.md): put one page from your box on the public web, with an explicit go-live and takedown.
