Nana Odette's Lemon Chicken

Serves 4. About an hour, mostly waiting.

Ingredients
- 8 chicken thighs, bone in, skin on
- 2 lemons — one juiced, one sliced thin
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon salt, plus more at the end
- black pepper
- 1 cup chicken stock
- a handful of parsley, chopped

Method
1. Toss the thighs with the lemon juice, garlic, oil, oregano, salt and
   pepper. Let them sit 20 minutes on the counter.
2. Heat a wide pan. Lay the thighs in skin down and leave them alone for
   8 minutes, until the skin lets go on its own.
3. Turn them, tuck the lemon slices around, pour in the stock.
4. Cover, low heat, 25 minutes. Uncover for the last 10 so the sauce tightens.
5. Parsley over the top. Salt again at the end, it always needs it.

Notes
Wren will only eat this if the lemon slices come out before serving, so fish
them out into a bowl and let everyone else add their own back.
Doubles fine, but use two pans — crowding steams the skin instead of crisping.
Good with rice, better with bread for the sauce.
