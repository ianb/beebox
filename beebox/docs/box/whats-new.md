# What's new in Bee Box

Changes you can notice, newest first. Each is marked **New** (something to
see or use), **Changed** (something that works differently), or **Agent**
(something you see only through what the box's agent does). The list started
on 2026-11-16, with the week before it filled in. Ask the box about any of
them.

- 2026-11-15 **New**: When you write with the box in the composition editor, an Accept control marks the agent's text in your draft as your own.
- 2026-11-14 **Agent**: When the box makes you something interactive, such as a practice problem, it can watch how you do and offer a hint when you seem stuck, without you asking.
- 2026-11-13 **New**: A shared chat shows when someone else is typing or dictating.
- 2026-11-12 **New**: You can start a voice call from a chat you share with other box members. Anyone in the call can turn on video or transcription.
- 2026-11-12 **New**: When another box member has the same chat open, their picture appears next to your profile menu.
- 2026-11-12 **Agent**: Ask the box for a tour, and it shows the steps in a bar across the top of the page that stays in view as you move around your box.
- 2026-11-10 **Agent**: The box can show math and chemistry as typeset formulas in chat and cards. Ask for an equation and you see it written out properly.
- 2026-11-10 **Agent**: A reminder the box sends you, such as a nightly "time to journal", opens a chat in the place it is about, such as your journal.
- 2026-11-09 **New**: When you leave something unfinished in a place, the box notes your next step. When you come back, that step shows at the top, with its card open.
