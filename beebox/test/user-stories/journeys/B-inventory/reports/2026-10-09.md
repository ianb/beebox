### Photo intake quality

Compared against the two journey photos, by item category.

- **Coverage.** Every item category visible in both photos has a record, grouped by
  drawer. One category was missed.
- **Counts.** Single-item counts are correct. Where a category appears in both
  drawers, the agent found and reported the second group, and the counts are
  correct. Group counts carry "roughly" in the value. Where a record notes a lower
  bound, it matches what is visible.
- **Misidentifications.** None stated with confidence. Three uncertain items were
  flagged in the first reply; the person settled all three.
- **Counts in a structured field.** Changed since 2026-10-08. The agent read
  `card-record.md` (transcript 11:45:24) and wrote each item as a `record` with
  `location: {text, ref}` and `quantity: {value, note?}`. 19 of 33 values are a
  bare number; the rest are unit phrases ("1 pack of 8") or "roughly N". The tray
  list repeats each count in prose. After the person asked, every record body
  also repeats both facts (row 26). The briefing now tells the agent to update
  the frontmatter, the body, and the list together (three copies of one count).
- **Shape.** One `doc` per container (`prominence: primary`), one `record` per
  item, a landmark for the area, a briefing for the rules. The record schema's
  own guidance names this case: "An inventory item needs `location` and
  `quantity` but probably no body" (`S/schemas/record.tsx:124`).

Against `watch-for.md`: the agent did not invent a card type; it used `record`.
It asked what the photos were for before it filed them. Counts are hedged where
the photo cannot settle them. Every cold answer named where the item is, with
links, and said that only the tray is inventoried so far.
