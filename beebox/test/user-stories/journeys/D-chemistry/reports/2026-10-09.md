## Harness defects

- **H1 — screenshots hang while the display sleeps.** All screenshot attempts (about a dozen, including after `bin/browse close`) failed with `CDP command timed out: Page.captureScreenshot` or an empty exit; snapshot, click and fill worked. The coordinator confirmed the cause: the Mac display had gone to sleep overnight, and capture works with the display held awake. Screenshots worked in session `aa-d2`. Consequences: no visual evidence, `spanMinutes: 0` (H5), and UI claims read from accessible names (H4). Documented in `beebox/test/user-stories/journeys/README.md` (`95073661c`); `prepare.ts` does not check for it. This answers the open question in [`browse-screenshot-hangs-while-snapshot-works`](../../../../../../issues/bugs/2026-09-20-browse-screenshot-hangs-while-snapshot-works.md); re-encounter note added 2026-10-09. Not fixed in tooling.
- **H2 — the browse auth cookie expires 30 minutes after the last `open`.** `browse/src/worktree.ts:166` sets the expiry; only `open` reseeds it (`browse/src/cli.ts:146-151`). A walk that stays on one page for 30 minutes loses auth, and the app's "session ended" bar appears (rows 37-40, 64, 74). Not filed: fixed in `9444051a6` (browse re-seeds the cookie on every command; `browse-auth-cookie-expires-mid-walk`).
