## What the walk achieved

The saved files support the account. Six commits created and revised one
`_content/todos/Reconnecting.doc.card`, with four fictional friends and one
inline todo. A separate new person schema or collection was unnecessary for
this evening's goal. The chat directory remained untracked; the friends
document was committed.

Marisol's Saturday dinner invitation is recorded as sent. Her Sunday lunch
counteroffer is recorded as received. The person's reply is explicitly **not
sent**, and no time or place is agreed. The obsolete Saturday suggestion is
historical context, not a competing current arrangement. No calendar entry
was created. The todo now asks for the reply, without a due date.

Noor is explicitly next, with a settled but unsent message, no deadline and no
follow-up if she is quiet. Idris's job change remains a question. Bram's summer
message history stays unknown rather than becoming a claim that he ignored
the person. The final drafts use the person's requested wording. These facts
were checked in the final file and its earlier commits, not inferred from the
assistant's assurances.

The person left for a blank browser page, returned through the original link,
and found the conversation restored. They also found the saved document via
Search and the task's origin link. This is an observed same-session return,
not evidence of a successful return weeks later.

The walker would probably keep using it as a place to hold context and words.
They preferred conversation to manipulating pages, and supplied much of the
final wording themselves. The product helped them advance a personal goal;
it did not merely produce a list of contacts. Sending messages outside the
app was a supplied fictional event, not a tested integration or a real send.

## Timing and cost

The first-to-last screenshot span was **14.5 minutes**. This includes reading,
tool operation and notes, so it is not product latency. Collection counted
135 note lines (including headings, blank lines and the terminal split entry).

Supported Claude root-chat transcripts yielded six user-to-first-text
intervals: **3.1 minutes total, median 31 seconds, slowest 47 seconds**. First
text can precede the actual answer. Scoped chats, other engines and full
completion are not covered. The walker was informed of this limitation during
the run; earlier timing remarks in its notes must be read with that correction.
Screenshot gaps over 45 seconds are instrument intervals, not measured waits.
The host reported memory pressure during post-walk test scheduling, so this
run is not a quiet-host performance benchmark.

One fresh walker drove the journey; a separate worker repaired the harness,
Opus reviewed the scenario and harness, and a source-reading agent helped
verify search/tool behavior. Dollar cost was not measured. Preparation spent
no chat turn proving readiness; the app's six measured exchanges were part of
the walk.

## Harness changes and limits

Before the walk, preparation still assumed a retired nested `content/` layout,
old CLI verbs and uppercase slugs. It now uses canonical fresh engine init,
current root paths, annex and installed commit hooks. It checks structured
health and actual app navigation before spending a walker. It retains old
evidence and still refuses another walk of a journey with unreported notes.
The composed prompt excludes `watch_for`.

Review then isolated preparation's browser session, reused canonical transcript
path helpers for collision protection, and made partial clock coverage explicit.
The current run's prompt stayed frozen; the walker received the timing
correction separately. There was no concurrent preparation during this walk.
The final harness changes passed eight selected doctest assertions, lint,
user-stories typecheck, doc checks and independent fix verification. The named
preparation browser session was code-reviewed after the live initial prepare;
it was not exercised by provisioning a second box.

The new scenario's review removed advance product-capability claims and an
analyst-like closing checklist. All people are from the fictional example
roster. No photographs, personal box contents or live messaging accounts were
used. The unmerged lending branch was inspected but not recovered or changed.

## Not verified

- Real external sending, delivery, replies or address-book integration.
- Weeks of continued use, reminder delivery, voice transcription, or TTS. The
  disposable box lacked voice/TTS secret grants; text was the chosen path.
- Full answer-completion latency, mobile layouts, or a human newcomer's patience.
- The precise cause of Browse returning at action 11, and native pointer-wheel
  behavior inside the pane. Successful heading navigation is not a wheel test.
- Cmd-K's section behavior in the browser; its omission was source-verified.

The four filed defects remain open. No product fixes were implemented. The
useful outcome and the confusing reading/search surfaces both belong in the
record.
