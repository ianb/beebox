## What the walk achieved

The box has three new commits, adding **eight cards and three Markdown files**
(the course instructions, runner notes and session log). `b93bf20` created a Chemistry Foundations course
after the opening probe; `5933b49` created the Three Dials recap, corrected the
isotope explanation, and recorded progress; `5ba2d14` recorded the second visit,
formula work, and the first two balancing exercises. The notes say the walker
answered isotope/ion questions, returned through the original link, read the
saved recap, explained subscripts and coefficients, and balanced the first two
small equations. Inspection of the final cards confirms the exercise answers,
closed formula task, and new cold-check task. A separate filesystem check found
one untracked chat card and three untracked memory files; their contents were
not part of this study-state verification. `collect.ts` itself records only the
two uncommitted directory paths.

The rich course model was reached through an ordinary description of the
person’s study problem: course, concept map, teaching plan, lesson plan and
evidence-backed progress were actually created. Discovering the schema names
was not a prerequisite for starting useful work.

The person left with a workable next visit: after a few days, explain isotope
versus ion without notes, try `N₂ + H₂ → NH₃`, and then study rusting. They would
keep using the app alongside a textbook, cautiously. The teaching interaction
and the saved short recaps were useful; tutor-facing cards, narrow panels,
implementation-looking labels, a stale outstanding exercise, and malformed
equation markup cost attention.

The person still counts slowly when balancing and has not studied moles or
quantitative reaction calculations. Rusting remains a next topic rather than a
completed explanation. The closing response also acknowledged that the
oxygen-18 ice-core claim in the recap was currently unsourced and that the app
owed the person an authoritative reference; that claim must not be treated as
verified chemistry evidence from this walk.

model (`schemas/progress.ts`). The final record rates isotopes and formula reading
solid, ions and balancing working, and leaves delayed recall untested. Its old
“Where they are” paragraph nevertheless says the already graded exercise is
outstanding. That contradiction is authored saved state, not a stale browser cache.

independently classified as a stale-current-state defect.

The false neutrality rule was checked against IUPAC’s [isotope definition](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/I03331)
and [ion definition](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/I03158): isotope identity
and net charge are independent properties. The saved recap corrects the error.
This is a narrow correctness check, not an audit of the whole chemistry lesson.
The transcript records the build-course skill being invoked before teaching;
its existing grounding instruction (`core/box/skills-content.ts:134–139`) did
not prevent the error. The acknowledged unsourced ice-core example is linked
as related grounding evidence, without calling it false.

The narrower recap is a preview nested inside The Plate: `TodoViewCard.tsx:132`
caps the parent at `max-w-2xl`; `todo-view/CardRow.tsx:60–69` puts FileEntry beside
its count stack; `ui/FileEntry.tsx:191–220` keeps the expanded FileView inside that
flex item. Focusing the outer workspace is therefore not opening the recap as a
full-width standalone document. Readability cost is preserved; a failed focus
control is not claimed.
