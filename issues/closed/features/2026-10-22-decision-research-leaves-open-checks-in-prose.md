Resolved 2026-10-22 by the journey-walks-late-oct fix commit. Guide TODOS: offer to make pre-decision checks todos with the deadline. Knowledge audit passed.

When I am deciding something over a few weeks, I want the few things that I still have to check in one short list, so that I can do them and know when I am ready to buy.

In the I-deciding walk the agent recorded four open checks: measure the 85 cm gap, ask about spin noise, confirm Saturday delivery, and confirm the delivery fee. They were in prose across the chat, the shortlist card, and the notes card. The person's top wish: "a place showing what is still open as a short to-do list for me … It is scattered across the chat and two pages." The box has todos (the root page lists "Todos"). The agent made none, and nothing marks the deadline of about 5 November.

Report: [I-deciding](../../../beebox/test/user-stories/journeys/I-deciding/reports/2026-10-22.md) (row 44, R17).

## Why the fix is not obvious

Decide whether the agent guide should say: when work leaves the person with checks to do, write them as todos with the deadline. Too many todos from every chat is the opposite failure; the [near-duplicate todos](../../features/2026-09-20-near-duplicate-todos-accumulate-across-cards.md) issue shows it.
