Alan Watts's temperament taxonomy: **prickly** people want rigor, particles,
precision; **gooey** people want flow, wholeness, vibes — and his claim that
the universe is irreducibly *prickly-goo*.

beebox is an unusually pure instance: maximum-strictness machinery
(strictest-mode YAML, verbatim span checks, fail-closed auth, lint-as-law,
types-over-docs) existing entirely in service of the gooiest possible
material — voice memos, half-thoughts, a human life's unstructured stream.
The prickles protect the goo; neither side is the point alone.

Boxholder flagged this as worth possible discussion (2026-07-22) — candidate
uses: a story/positioning frame for the front-door site (it names the
system's central tension in one image), or a lens for design conversations
about where strictness belongs and where it strangles. A nugget-shaped idea
that came from conversation rather than extraction.
