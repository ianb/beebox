The boxholder's game concept: an infinite series of mini-games, with some
overall structure and some directions in which the series develops. This
is a concept to keep, not a request to build it.

It belongs with the [game framework
discussion](2026-10-26-box-game-framework-plugin.md), where an outer game
holds bounded mini-game encounters and the agent writes new ones. In this
concept the series has no end: the agent keeps producing the next
mini-game, and the structure gives the sequence a shape so it is not a
random stream.

## Open questions

- What is the overall structure: a map, a story, a progression of skills,
  something else? Is it fixed at the start or does it grow with the series?
- What are the "development directions": themes, mechanics, difficulty,
  subject matter? Does the player choose a direction, does play choose it,
  or does the agent?
- How does one mini-game lead to the next: does the next one build on what
  the player did, reuse its pieces, or vary one rule?
- Where do the mini-games come from: generated new each time, remixed from
  the library, or both? How is quality kept up over an unbounded series?
- How does an endless series fit the person-sets-the-terms rule from the
  framework discussion (limits, stopping points), so "infinite" does not
  become an engagement trap?
