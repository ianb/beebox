# alien-polyline — cycle notes

## Cycle 1
**Intent:** First full draft. Every outline is a polygon/move-line path built from sampled ellipses (~1 vertex per 3px), a Catmull-Rom sampler (12 points per span), and a tube() offsetter for the arms. Order: shadow, antennae, legs/feet, arms, body, head, eyes, cheeks, smile. Flat fills with ink outlines (polygon carries both fill and brushed stroke). Shading: dark-green bristle strokes clipped to the body's right side; a diagonal pencil hatch on the lower right of the head plus a light-green bristle highlight on the upper left of the head.
**Seen:** Close to the guide in pose and proportion; ink outlines read as smooth curves, no visible facets at this size. Problems: (1) a small step in the head outline at its rightmost point (~305,173), where the polygon starts and ends (the ellipse sampler starts at angle 0); probably the wobble not meeting itself at the seam. (2) The left arm's shoulder end pokes out of the body as a pointed ink spike at ~(143,285) because the tube's start is outside the body outline. (3) The shading barely shows: the pencil hatch on the head is a faint set of thin parallel diagonals (visible in a zoom, nearly invisible at 1x), the bristle highlight on the head is faint, and the body's bristle shadow reads only as a few faint vertical streaks. The picture is still mostly flat colour.

## Cycle 2
**Intent:** Move every oval's start point to its bottom (angle pi/2) to hide the closing seam; start both arm centerlines inside the body so the shoulder ends are covered; make shading visible: wider (10px) dark-green bristle strokes with "swell" pressure down the body's right side and under the head, light-green bristle strokes down the body's left, head pencil hatch opacity 0.5 -> 0.8 with a second crossing pass in the lower right, head highlight bristle 10px/0.6 -> 14px/0.8.
**Seen:** The head seam step is gone (right edge clean in a 3x zoom), and the left arm no longer pokes out at the shoulder; both fixes worked. Shading is now visible: dark-green bristle streaks down the body's right edge and a shadow band under the head; the pale left-side strokes read as two thin pointed light smears, a little like reflections on plastic. The head hatch is still faint and its straight diagonal lines ignore the round form. I also noticed that the shading strokes are drawn after the outline inside the clip, so they paint over the inner half of the ink line; the outline looks slightly thinner where shading crosses it.

## Cycle 3
**Intent:** Restructure each part as fill -> clipped shading -> ink outline on top, so shading never covers the line. Replace straight hatching with form-following marks: concentric bristle and pencil arcs inset from the head's lower-right rim, a light bristle arc on the upper-left of the head; dark bristle strokes along the underside of each arm (offset from the arm centerline), down the right of each leg, along the bottom of each foot and hand, and a crescent on the belly; an orange-yellow pencil arc on the antenna balls; a pale grey pencil arc for the eye socket shadow; cheeks as soft pink pencil strokes instead of flat ovals.
**Seen:** The head now has real volume: curved dark-green bristle bands follow the lower-right rim, with pencil arcs inside them, and a pale arc on the upper left. The outlines stay a constant full weight everywhere (the fill -> shade -> outline order fixed the thinning). The belly has a faint darker crescent on its right. Regressions and misses: the cheeks almost vanished; the pink pencil strokes are a few broken dotted lines, far lighter than the flat ovals they replaced. The shading on the arms, legs, feet and hands is invisible at 1x and barely there at 2.5x; GREEN_DARK at 0.6 opacity over GREEN has too little contrast on short marks. The antenna-ball pencil arc is not visible.

## Cycle 4
**Intent:** Restore the cheeks as flat pink ovals with two short darker-pink pencil strokes over them for texture. Make limb shading read: darken GREEN_DARK (#4f9a45 -> #3f8538), raise dark-bristle opacity 0.6 -> 0.75 and set load 1.5 so short marks do not dry out. Antenna-ball shading: switch to a darker orange (#c78a1a) bristle stroke, 5px.
**Seen:** Cheeks are back and read well: flat pink ovals with a faint darker texture line. The body's right-side shadow is darker and streakier, now clearly bristle; the shadow under the head on the body is visible. The antenna-ball shading is still not readable at 1x. The left arm now has a visible darker band down its inner side in a 5x zoom, faint at 1x. The leg shading is barely there: the stroke at x-3 is mostly hidden under the 3.2px outline, so only a sliver shows. Overall the figure looks shaded on head and body but the limbs still look flat.

## Cycle 5
**Intent:** Limb shading: move the leg stroke inward (x-6) and widen to 10px, widen arm-underside strokes to 11px and move them toward the centre (offset 6->4 and 7->5) so less is hidden under the outline, shrink the hand arcs to radius 8 so they sit inside. Add pencil texture on the body: six curved pencil strokes following the right side (x 214-239). Ground shadow: paler flat oval plus seven grey-brown pencil strokes, longest in the middle, for a scribbled shadow.
**Seen:** The limb changes helped: each leg now has a visible darker stripe on its right, the arms show a darker band on their shadow side, the hands a darker crescent. The body pencil texture shows as fine vertical grain lines on the right half, which reads as hand-drawn shading. The ground shadow is worse: the paler fill plus thin pencil lines reads as a faint smudge with less shape than cycle 4's flat oval; the scribbles are only visible up close. The light streaks on the body's left are still two pointed pale smears. Antenna balls still show no shading at 1x.

## Cycle 6
**Intent:** Final polish. Ground shadow: darken the flat oval back toward cycle 4 (#ddd6c3) and keep the pencil scribbles on top. Body highlight: replace the straight light streak with one 9px light-green bristle stroke following the body's left curve. Antenna balls: add a small cream highlight dot at the upper left so they read as spheres.
**Seen:** The antenna balls now read as small shiny spheres (cream dot upper left); that helped and is visible at 1x. The ground shadow has its shape back: a defined beige oval with faint pencil scribbles across it, between cycle 4's flat oval and cycle 5's smudge. The left-body highlight is now one curved pale stroke following the body edge; at 1x it is a subtle sheen, at 2.5x it still has a slightly pointed bristle end, but it no longer looks like two separate smears. The overall picture: clean, even ink outlines with no visible facets; head and body shaded with bristle and pencil; limbs lightly shaded. Still flatter and more "vector" than a true picture-book painting.
