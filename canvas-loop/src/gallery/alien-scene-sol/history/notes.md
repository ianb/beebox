## Cycle 1

**Intent:** Compose a twilight meadow with a large friendly alien waving to a small departing ship. Build colored atmosphere, textured ground, and varied organic contours before judging the first render.

**Seen:** The story is legible and the head and ship hold attention, but the sky has evenly spaced horizontal bristle bands that look like striped wallpaper. The close crops show pleasing broken bristle edges in the face and body; the bag is readable. The meadow is thin, with little overlap around the feet.

## Cycle 2

**Intent:** Lower the sky brush opacity and interrupt its bands with quiet clouds. Add a scarf and near plants to make the character and hill feel more inhabited, while sharpening the tiny passenger's wave.

**Seen:** The sky recedes much better and the yellow scarf gives the alien a sense of motion. The passenger's tiny raised arm reads in the ship crop. The moon and distant hills still look like single flat color cutouts, and the alien's near hand reads as a round mitten.

## Cycle 3

**Intent:** Brush subtle texture onto the moon and hill layers, add foreground depth, and draw small finger separations so the raised hand communicates a wave.

**Seen:** The distant hill strokes add soft layers and the moon has a faint mottled surface; both help without taking focus. The hand crop shows that two short lines are too slight to turn the mitten into individual fingers. At full size the gesture still reads as a friendly wave, and the scene feels complete enough to stop rather than add distracting detail.
