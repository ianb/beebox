# Nell Rows Out, animated: notes

## Cycle 1
**Intent:** Port the still page (open-opus-a) to TEA. Paint the static page into five cached layers (background keyed on dusk; boat back + girl; boat front + oar; carp head; lily pads). Each frame draws only the moving parts: flickering lantern and its glow, sway on the bail, shimmering reflection glints, carp rings spreading in a 180-frame loop, chin drops, carp mouth breathing, blink, eye following the lantern, moths circling, twinkling stars, reeds leaning, and a lantern-centred darkening that deepens with dusk. Moving marks are filled polygon "ribbons" instead of ink marks (the reeds alone cost 17 ms as ink). Interactions: drag the lantern within Nell's reach, click the carp to greet it, click water for a ripple. Params dusk, wind, moths, reset. Events demonstrate each.
**Seen:** Every event engaged (touch-water, greet-carp, take-lantern, move-lantern x3, let-go); the stray mouseups read (unhandled), which is true. The page keeps its painted look: the cached layers are pixel-identical to the still, and the moving ribbons sit in it without looking vector. The touch ripple reads as rings on water. The lifted lantern hangs from the mitten and its reflection column follows it. At dusk 0.8 the sky and water darken, more stars appear, and the lantern-centred darkening leaves Nell, the boat and the carp in the light. Problem: the greeted blink (frame 44, zoom 2x) reads as a missing eye, because the lid is the head colour with no lash line. Cost: about 13 ms per frame after a 1.2 s first frame (61- and 121-frame runs against a 1-frame run).

## Cycle 4

## Cycle 5
**Intent:** Two motion upgrades. (1) Dusk: cache the painted sky and water at three dusks (0, 0.35, 1) and cross-fade the two that bracket the current value with a group opacity, so moving the dusk slider is smooth and never repaints (a new value used to cost a 0.5 s repaint and a cache slot). (2) The lantern swings: when Nell's hand moves, the lantern lags and then swings on its bail as a damped pendulum (amplitude from the hand's sideways speed, decay 45 frames). Same events; the drag at frames 60-74 now leaves a swing to see at 75-90.
**Seen:** Diff of frame 96 against cycle 4 (which painted dusk 0.8 directly): only the lantern region changed (the new swing), so the cross-fade is indistinguishable from a real repaint at 1x. Zoom 2x frames 75 and 88: after the drag the lantern hangs tilted and swings back, with the bail following. Cost: about 12 ms per frame at the default dusk and about 16 ms between anchors (the group for the fade); the machine is noisy, so these are best-of-two. The moths at 2x read as paper triangles. The bow's glow stays where the lantern rested even when the lantern is lifted.
