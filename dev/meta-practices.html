How the practices accreted

Not what got built — how they learned to build it. Time runs top to bottom; each dot on the spine is a development practice landing in the repo. The spacing is the shape: where practices cluster it's a burst of method-building; the long quiet stretch in the middle is heads-down shipping with no new process. Colored by the kind of practice.

Dates are first-commit (some predate the May monorepo merge; original commit dates survive in history). The agent-native practices — knowledge audits, the no-disable guardrail, cross-model review, skills, the self-improvement meta-instruction — are quality inventions specific to developing with agents.
