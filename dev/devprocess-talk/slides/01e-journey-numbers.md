# What the walks produced
## Supporting: three rounds, numbers from git
- 19 walks in four rounds: Aug 23–25, Sep 20–21, Oct 8, Oct 9
- 1,321 claim rows → 98 issues; 100 rows were the harness, not the product
- 50 closed (33 the same day, by the walk's own stream), 48 open; 8 wait on a human
- Oct 9 confirmed every Oct 8 fix it reached, and found the defect the fixes introduced

Notes:
Full treatment, with the commands behind each number: dev/journeys/overview.md. The repeat count is the crude measure of what did not get fixed between rounds. The contrast with the test suite is the point: a walk finds product questions that need a human decision; a suite finds regressions an agent can fix.
Caveat to say aloud: the walker is an agent under evaluation, and such agents can go around the thing being evaluated. After-run review catches most of that inside the box; what it cannot see is the walker going outside the box to get its result.
