# The walker who knows nothing
## Cold open: handing the hardest job to an agent
- To test the product as a new user, I had to forget what I built, then do the walking
- I could do neither well, so an agent plays a person with one goal and no product words
- First walk: three of the seven bugs it reported were faults in my test setup
- The fix to that is the pattern of this talk: the relief exposed the next constraint

Notes:
This is bottleneck 5 in the sequence, but it is the clearest single illustration, so it opens the talk. The supporting slides show the journey definition, the walker prompt, a walk report row, the after-action rule, and the numbers.
