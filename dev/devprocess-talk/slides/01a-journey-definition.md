## Supporting: journey.yaml for "I never know what I've got"

- One field never reaches the walker: what the observer is watching for

```yaml
id: B-inventory
title: I never know what I've got
situation: |
  Your workroom has a tray of odds and ends — craft bits, small tools, stationery,
  things accumulated over years. You never know what you have. You bought a glue
  stick last month and then found three. You spent ten minutes last week hunting
  for a carabiner you were certain you owned.
watch_for: |
  There is no inventory or item card type, so this tests whether the box can be bent
  to a shape the person brought. Watch whether it offers one, invents one, asks, or
  files the photos somewhere generic and considers the matter closed.
```
