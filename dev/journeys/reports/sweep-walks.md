## 1. What a journey is and intends

The plan opens by naming what the catalog cannot do: "[The 2026-08-21 catalog] is an inventory: 649 statements of the form 'the product can do X'. It has no notion of a person with a motive." (`PLAN:14-15`). A journey instead is "a goal from someone's life with a reason attached, because the reason decides what a good outcome looks like" (`PLAN:21-22`). The README restates the split: the catalog "answers 'does the product do X'; this answers 'can someone actually use it for something they wanted'" (`J/README.md:4-6`). The plan's central prediction is that the usual outcome is not wrong behaviour but "**data turns up and the box has no shape to put it in**" (`PLAN:85`), which it calls "the ordinary condition of a box that has not been taught anything yet" (`PLAN:90`), and so a subject of the journey rather than a failure (`PLAN:93`). The reader is to watch whether the box notices, asks, proposes a shape, or files the thing somewhere generic (`PLAN:95-100`).
